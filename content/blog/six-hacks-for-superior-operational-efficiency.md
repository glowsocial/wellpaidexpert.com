The more you can achieve operational efficiency, the more you can serve yourself and your customers. When business runs smoothly, everyone benefits.

But how can you do this? What hacks are out there for enhanced operations?

That’s what we discuss in this post. We look at some shortcuts that most businesses miss, but that you should use.

## Streamline Meetings

Our first hack is to streamline meetings. You want to make sure that you’re setting various time limits and actionable outcomes.

Often in businesses, meetings have a tendency to run on. In general, people like talking to each other and discussing their requirements.

If you can put a limit on this, that can be helpful. It ensures colleagues use their time productively, and don’t waste it chatting to others

## Use Cloud-Based Collaboration Tools

At the same time, you also want to use
cloud-based collaboration tools. These make it so much easier for people to talk to each other.

For example, Google Workspace and Notion both allow real-time collaboration. Two employees can literally work on the same document at the same time, with no issues.

Collaboration can also take the form of chat apps instead of clunky email tools. These are often significantly more efficient (and easy to use).

## Add Inventory Space

Another way to improve operations quickly is to add some
small warehouse space. Having these facilities available is handy and prevents stock items from having to be stored at directors’ homes.

These days, finding flexible inventory space is easy. Countless businesses are now offering it, meaning that surges in demand are easier to cope with when they come.

## Standardise Operating Procedures

Next, you’ll want to think about how you can standardise your operating procedures to make them more efficient. The basic goal should be to automate all recurring tasks, or turn them into a procedure your people can follow.

Once you do this with
most of the functions in your business, you should find that it ticks along better. You can also implement checklists that staff can follow that reduce the number of errors they commit.

## Use Predictive Maintenance

Predictive maintenance could also be helpful if you rely on machinery and production processes. AI and statistical techniques can tell you when you need to service something before it breaks down, preventing stoppages.

For example, a lot of companies now use vibration sensors on machinery that can detect subtle changes in internal mechanisms. Evidence suggests that these can
cut maintenance
requirements by around half or more in some situations.

## Tech Will Help You

If you want superior operational efficiency, you’re going to need to embrace the fact that we now live in a tech dominated world. If you can’t do that, you’re never going to see the success that you desire.

Standard tech is going to be good, but you need more than that too. You need to go beyond, with things like [power dialers](https://www.withallo.com/product/power-dialer) to help you get through your call list each day. It’s sometimes the little things that make the most difference, and you need to take advantage of them when they come along.

## Batch Tasks

Finally, batching tasks is one of the best ways to ensure more seamless operations. Doing all your emails and meeting scheduling at the same time is a great way to free you up for other things.

Sometimes, productivity experts call this “block timing” but the idea is the same. When you have the
luxury of focusing
on just one thing, you tend to get it done faster.

For example, when you do all your emails in one go, you reduce the cognitive cost of switching to other tasks.

Use these hacks to help your business run more smoothly.
