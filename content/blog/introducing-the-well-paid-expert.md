# Introducing: The Well-Paid Expert

Hi, I’m Kathleen.

I’ve gone through so many pivots.

We entrepreneurs have a lot of shame around pivots.

Because there’s some sort of stigma around failing, and failure and pivoting and changing things.

As if somehow we think entrepreneurs should get things right the first time, when that’s not how humans work.

I’m here to stop that.

This is my next pivot. The Well-Paid Expert.

And there’s not much on it, not yet.

But it’s my new home.

## Why “The Well-Paid Expert,” anyway?

Because every single one of us is an expert in their field.

Everyone, that is, who has lived a bit of life.

Who has developed their areas of expertise.

Who is finished with conventional schooling, but never done learning.

So really, you.

Me.

All of us.

And it pains me to see people who have
so much expertise
fail. Or rather, not fail, necessarily, because there’s no shame in failing. But undercharging.

Or worse, leaving the entrepreneurial space altogether because they’ve internalized the falsehood that there’s no room in this market for their expertise.

## And I want to change that.

I want to show you how to make a decent living online.

There is
so much money
out here, on the internet, just waiting for you to take it.

The pie, as it is, is infinite.

And there’s room for you, too.

I hope you join me.

I have a
Facebook group.

A
LinkedIn group.

And no, I’m not well versed in running groups, so join them with your expectations reasonably set.

I’m really excited.

I’m looking forward to seeing where we can take this.

And I’d love for you to come with me.
