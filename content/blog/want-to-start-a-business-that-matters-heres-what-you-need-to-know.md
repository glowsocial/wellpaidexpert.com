The internet is full of “start a business “ ideas and nonsense that has been rinsed through and have no value anymore for new modern businesses. The reality is the world is drowning in Shopify stores and 3PL businesses that are set up as a side hustle, and cutting through noise is hard and near impossible for those new to the game.

If you really want to pour your heart, soul, and energy into a new venture, you need to at least make sure it’s not just clogging up an already saturated space and you’re building something that matters and makes a difference.

## Start With Everyday People Problems

You really need to be starting a business that people need, filling a gap that is missing and providing value to people’s lives. It’s things like finding the solution to expensive childcare woes for struggling families, it’s delivering tutoring services for kids falling through the cracks, and it’s finding care solutions for seniors in your local area that help out in a way they’re not able to access right now.

You need to be determining what needs real people so you can solve their problems. For example,
starting a care agency
means you get people on the ground to help your local community with support where and when they need it.

## Forget The Quick Wins

Meaningful businesses don’t shoot up overnight; they take time to get things right. To build the foundations for something that has gravity and can solve problems. You’re not chasing the unicorn or reinventing the wheel. And this means slower to market and building loyalty the hard way. It’s about showing people the difference you can make so they’ll stick around and trust you.

## People Who Care

You need to
build your business with people who care, so you can reinforce what you’re doing and build heart and soul into the company. Because if you want to make a difference, you need others who are on the same page as you. Surround yourself with employees, business partners and peers who are in the game for the right reasons and help you bring the energy you need, and who aren’t just waiting for the paycheck at the end of the week.

## People Not Profit

Your business needs to be financially viable, but when you’re doing something to make a difference, you need the focus to be on people, not profits. If all you care about is squeezing every penny out of people, they will notice and they will go elsewhere. Businesses that matter put people first. It’s for the families relying on you to provide a service, the staff who need the wage to support their families, and the community that is giving you a chance. And if you betray their trust, it won’t end well.

If you really want to build a business that has legs and can go the distance and be something people genuinely want and need, then you need to be careful with how you approach it and make sure you’re in for all the right reasons.
