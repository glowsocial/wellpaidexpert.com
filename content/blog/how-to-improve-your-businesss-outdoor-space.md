Having outdoor space at your business premises is a huge advantage. While it may be a great feature, having outdoor areas at your business means that it’s essential you take care to keep them looking at their best. You see, the outdoor space around your business plays a crucial role in your building’s curb appeal and helps to make an immediate
impression
of your company. Ensuring that the first impression you give clients is a positive one is a must. However, understanding how to improve your business’s outdoor space so that it looks at its best and is guaranteed to impress your customers isn’t always easy. If you’re looking for some ideas and inspiration, keep reading to find out how you can improve your business’s outdoor space.

## Decide on the Purpose of Your Outdoor Space

Before you do anything else, it’s important to decide on the purpose of your business’s outdoor space. Do you intend your outdoor area to be used for your
staff
as a relaxation space while they’re on their breaks? Will your outdoor space be used by customers? Do you plan to host
events
in the space?

The purpose of the space and how it will be used will heavily influence the layout of the space, and the elements that need to be incorporated within the design, for example seating and covered areas.

Having a strong idea of how the space will be used ensures that it will perfectly match the requirements of your business, and that the final design creates the impression you are looking for.

## Start With Selecting the Correct Surfacing

Once you have decided on the purpose of your outdoor space, it’s time to focus on the practical elements. Choosing the perfect surface for your new outdoor area is a must to ensure it can be used in the way you intend it to be.

Gravel is a popular choice for outdoor areas as it’s low maintenance, long-lasting, it looks attractive, and can help to improve drainage. But choosing the right type of gravel is essential to achieve these benefits. It’s helpful to look at a
guide to gravel sizes
to help you make your final decision. Once you know the size of gravel that’s most appropriate for your space, you can move on to selecting a color.

## Consider Your Brand

As your outdoor space will play a vital role in creating a first impression of your business, it’s essential that you ensure its aesthetic matches your brand. If your company sells luxury goods, you will want to ensure that your outside space reflects this high-end image. In contrast, if your brand is focused on being sustainable and eco-friendly, designing the space so that it aligns with this is a must.

## Final Thoughts

Improving the outdoor space surrounding your business premises is an excellent way to provide an enriching space for your team to relax in. However, the outdoor area is also a perfect opportunity to build a positive first impression of your business.
