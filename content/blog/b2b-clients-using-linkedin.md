## Why Try to Get B2B Clients Using LinkedIn

Contrary to popular belief, LinkedIn isn’t just for the ‘stodgy’. It’s often overlooked despite having more than 800 million members, with 4 out of 5 people on the platform driving business decisions. LinkedIn’s algorithm favors quality over quantity, allowing you to reach more people with less effort, given a strategic, people-focused approach.

And it’s completely different from the other social media networks out there, which favor an indirect approach.

On LinkedIn, it’s about conversations first. The direct approach. Content marketing (the indirect approach) only comes after you’ve started talking to people.

You can drive conversations towards a sales call, ensure your potential clients remember you and your expertise, and put your abilities on display by following a simple LinkedIn strategy.

## The Hidden Costs of Ignoring LinkedIn

Sure, LinkedIn might not have the hip factor of Instagram, the virality of TikTok, or the popularity of Facebook. But overlooking this platform, especially in the B2B consulting world, could come with a bigger price tag than you imagine.

## The Opportunity Cost

Think about it this way: while you’re figuring out your perfect hashtag strategy or crafting the ultimate tweet, your competition is potentially busy having productive, business-focused conversations on LinkedIn. And these aren’t just small talk. They’re connections that could easily lead to calls, meetings, partnerships, and eventually, contracts.

Every minute you spend neglecting LinkedIn is a minute your competitors might be using to secure your ideal clients. And when your competitors are successful in reaching out at scale, it’s not just one client you’re losing out on – it’s a whole pipeline.

## The Quality of Connection

LinkedIn isn’t just about quantity, it’s also about quality. Remember how I mentioned that 4 out of 5 LinkedIn members drive business decisions? These are the folks you’re missing out on every time you dismiss LinkedIn.

People on LinkedIn aren’t just idly scrolling through cute cat videos or holiday photos. They’re in a professional mindset, actively looking for solutions, new ideas, and yes, potentially your expert services. The quality of the connections you make on LinkedIn could be significantly higher than on other platforms, with a much greater conversion potential.

## The Compounding Losses

Ignoring LinkedIn isn’t a one-time loss; it’s a series of missed opportunities that can compound over time. Imagine all the valuable, high-quality connections you could make, all the insightful content you could share, and all the fruitful conversations you could start.

By neglecting to tailor your approach specifically for LinkedIn, you are not solely disregarding potential opportunities that are available at present, but also turning a blind eye to potential future prospects that can stem from starting new conversations.

## The Cost of Inaction

Above all, the most significant cost of neglecting LinkedIn is the cost of inaction. Sticking to old habits might seem like the comfortable choice, but comfort rarely equates to growth.

By adopting a proactive approach towards LinkedIn and acknowledging it as an essential aspect of your outreach plan, you can manage your lead generation efficiently, establish a more sustainable business, and ultimately become a highly sought-after authority in your domain.
