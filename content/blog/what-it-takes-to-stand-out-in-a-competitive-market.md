The internet has changed the business world forever. While you might be able to reach more people than ever before, you’re also sharing your market with more competitors than ever before. As such, it becomes vital to ensure that you’re able to stand out and make your voice heard. Here are a few methods that can help you.

Image – CC0 License

## Have A Sterling Online Presence

For most businesses, your website is going to be where you make your true first impression. Even if customers see an ad or a post, what awaits them when they click on it? Take the time to ensure that your
website truly serves your business. It should have a modern aesthetic design and format, with clear navigation to the content they’re most likely going to want to see. What’s more, responsive design is increasingly important in a space where mobile devices, such as smart phones and tablets, are used to browse the net more and more.

## Show That You’re An Expert

When people choose smaller businesses, they want to be sure that they’re dealing with those who know their business better than anyone. Aside from clear product and service pages on your site, the best way to display your insight is with the help of
content marketing. Coming up with a robust content plan that addresses many of the questions, concerns, and needs of your customers not only allows you to incorporate recommendations of your own products and services. It also helps you showcase your knowledge and how you can directly apply it to the customer’s wants and barriers they face.

## Focus On Customer Service

Another reason that customers will opt for smaller businesses is because they know that trying to get direct help from big corporations can feel like pulling teeth. As such, you should double-down on this perception, turning it into a strength with personalized and empathetic customer service, such as incorporating
live chat widgets
on your site that allow your team to directly address questions and concerns. You should also ensure that there’s some form of number for direct calls if you’re able. Being able to showcase the human side of your business can help you build a truly personal connection with customers.

## Aim For Innovation

If you’re simply content to keep offering the same things year after year, eventually, many of your customers are going to outgrow their needs for your products and services. By working to continuously improve your products, services, and processes, you can make sure that you’re always able to offer a strong alternative to your competitors. What’s more, you can develop new offerings that allow you to keep selling to repeat customers. If you can increase the maximum lifetime value per customer, then winning new customers can be less of a priority.

If you’re not able to offer the lowest prices, which small businesses rarely can, then you have to ensure that you’re able to stand out in other ways. Without it, you’re not going to be able to build the recognition nd trust that can help you thrive.
