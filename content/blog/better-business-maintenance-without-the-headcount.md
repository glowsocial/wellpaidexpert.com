Better business maintenance is possible without hiring more staff, which adds to an already sizable wage expense. With some minor adjustments to workplace processes, even the most unoptimized system can help ensure excellent standards are met for minimal productivity disruption. But what are these magical methods of improved efficiency? From automating the most repetitive tasks to using AI for data-driven forecasting, here are some suggestions.

## Use Third Parties for Specialist Skills

You have a core team of workers that provide results, but even then, you can't afford to hire experts for specific things. Fortunately, outsourcing is a valuable and much cheaper option when you need help without the headcount. For example, you don't need to hire a full-time engineer to maintain vital [cast iron caster](https://globalnexus.com.ph/how-do-you-maintain-cast-iron-caster-wheels/) wheels on trolleys and lifters. Although these are vital for office and industrial lifters and trolleys, they hardly ever break, so an outsourced service is best.

## Automate the Most Repetitive Tasks

Most modern businesses are no strangers to automation, but it's just a matter of how involved a company wants automated processes to be. Some companies rely almost entirely on it, while others get by with minimal automation. Either way, tools such as Cflow and ProcessMaker help companies improve efficiency by automating representative administrative tasks. This includes customer service and data entry, therefore reducing the need for manual human intervention.

## Better Business Maintenance with Optimized Workflows

Some recent studies have concluded that productivity isn't the issue at most businesses, and they suffer because over 90% of companies today have underdeveloped workflow management. Today, it is easier than you think to implement [superior operational efficiency](https://thewellpaidexpert.com/six-hacks-for-superior-operational-efficiency/) at your company. With adjustments such as mapping out processes to identify bottlenecks and breaking tasks down into sequential automated steps, everything becomes smoother.

## Use AI for Data-Driven Forecasting

AI is changing how companies are run, and many of the tools available come with genuine benefits for business. One of the most valuable is analysis, which AI tools perform extremely well. Faster and more capable than a human, AI tools can use data to predict many different things, including maintenance tasks, but also models that predict revenue changes. Over time and with extended use, AI tools will help superiors and teams make better decisions.

## Develop a Planned Preventive Maintenance Plan

Of course, the ideal method of improving ongoing productivity at a business is [creating a preventative maintenance plan](https://www.brightlysoftware.com/en-gb/blog/preventive-maintenance-plan). With a proactive approach, teams can identify when and where something is likely to happen and work on a solution before it even becomes a problem. Most companies today use specialist tools such as Computer-Aided Facilities Management (CFAM) tools that allow businesses to avoid unnecessary costs by reacting quickly to emergencies.

## Summary

It is recommended to use specialist third-party services for complex problems for better business maintenance without hiring more staff. It also helps to look at existing processes and analyze them to see where they can be improved for optimized workflows. However, you might get better results with a proactive approach through a preventative maintenance plan.
