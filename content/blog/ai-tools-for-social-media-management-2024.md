AI has changed the economics of social media management more than any other category. A one-person operator with the right tools can now serve the client load that used to require a 3–4 person team.

But the tool landscape is genuinely confusing — most products are either too generic (ChatGPT for everything) or too niche (tools that only do one thing). Here's what actually works.

## The Three Categories of Tools You Need

**1. Content Generation**
This is where AI has the biggest impact. Content generation tools draft captions, post ideas, long-form content, email copy, and ad creative.

| Tool | Best for | Cost |
|------|---------|------|
| Glow Social | Agency owners managing multiple clients with platform-specific content | $49–$199/mo |
| Claude (Anthropic) | Long-form content drafts, nuanced brand voice | $20/mo |
| ChatGPT Plus | General content ideation and drafting | $20/mo |

For agency use, purpose-built tools like [Glow Social](https://glowsocial.com) outperform general LLMs because they're optimized for the specific format requirements of each platform and designed for managing multiple brand voices.

**2. Scheduling and Publishing**
AI content tools handle creation; scheduling tools handle distribution. You need both.

| Tool | Best for | Cost |
|------|---------|------|
| Buffer | Simple scheduling across platforms | $6–$12/mo |
| Metricool | Multi-client management + analytics | $22–$50/mo |
| Later | Visual planning, Instagram-forward | $18–$40/mo |

For agency work with multiple clients, Metricool's multi-brand capability makes it worth the higher price. For personal brand management, Buffer is usually sufficient.

**3. Design and Visuals**
Text-only posts underperform on most platforms. Canva remains the best tool for fast, professional graphics at scale.

- **Canva Pro** ($13/month): Template-based design, AI image generation, brand kit management for multiple clients
- **Adobe Express** ($10/month): Good alternative if your clients are already in the Adobe ecosystem

## What AI Tools Can't Do (Yet)

AI tools reduce the time it takes to produce good content. They don't replace:

- **Judgment about what's appropriate for a client's brand.** Generic AI doesn't know your client's voice, their industry sensitivities, or what their audience has responded to before.
- **Trend awareness.** AI tools trained on historical data won't catch real-time cultural moments, breaking news, or emerging platform features.
- **Relationship management.** Community management — responding to comments and DMs — still requires human judgment.

The best agency operators use AI to handle production efficiency while applying their expertise to strategy, quality control, and client relationships.

## The One-Person Agency Tech Stack

For a solo operator managing 5–7 social media clients, here's a lean, effective stack:

| Function | Tool | Monthly cost |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Content creation | Glow Social | $99 |
| Scheduling | Metricool | $22 |
| Design | Canva Pro | $13 |
| Client reporting | Google Slides or Loom | $0–$15 |
| Communication | Slack or email | $0 |

**Total: ~$134–$149/month** against $7,500–$10,500 in monthly retainer revenue. That's a margin structure that makes a one-person agency genuinely profitable.

## Why the Tools Are Worth Understanding Before You Scale

Mastering the AI production stack before you have clients means your first client gets a polished, efficient service right from the start. Figuring out your workflow while managing 5 clients simultaneously leads to errors, delays, and churn.
