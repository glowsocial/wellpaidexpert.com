If you are in the process of
starting a business
then you may already know what your business needs to thrive. Something else you may be aware of is a high percentage of businesses won’t make it through to their fifth year of trading. There can be many reasons for this including running out of money and not having the knowledge needed to run a business. If you need some ideas on what your business needs then take a look at the article below.

## Funding

When you start a business you will need a solid chunk of money to do so. You may have heard previously that you can start a business with no funding. While this is true, it tends to just be businesses that are based online or one that have been handed down to another person. If you don’t fall into one of these categories then you are going to need to raise funds for your business another way. You will need to work out how much you need to start your business and apply for a business loan if you don’t have this to hand. The bank needs to be confident that you are able to make the monthly repayments.

## Employees

Another thing your business will need to survive and thrive is a great team of employees. When you have enough funding to pay salaries then you need to decide if you want employees, not all companies have them. However, they do make things a lot easier and take the weight off your shoulders. When the time comes to
hire employees
you need to ensure that you get the right people, they need to work well independently and as part of a team. If you don’t hire team players then your business could come tumbling down. Something else you need to be aware of when it comes to employees is a high churn rate. This happens when employees come and go in quick succession, leaving you without employees and taking valuable time away from your business.

## Website

Your business will need a strong, stable website in order to take orders when your main doors are shut. If your customers and clients are not able to navigate your site then they are likely to take their business elsewhere. This means you could be losing out on valuable customers and money just because your website isn’t as good as it could be. If you don’t know how to design a website then it might be worth speaking with a web design and development agency.

## Software

Finally, if you are looking for effective and efficient ways to run your business then there are plenty of apps and software that you can use. If you need to send invoices to customers and clients then you might make use of an accountant software rather than working everything out yourself. If you are running a healthcare service then it might be worthwhile investing in a
behavioral health EHR
for all your record keeping in one place.

We hope you found this article helpful and that it gave you some useful ideas and inspiration on some of the things you need to consider for your business. Getting everything right from the start means your business will be successful and stand the test of time.
