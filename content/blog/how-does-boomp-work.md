## Direct Answer

Boomp studies your website and business details, prepares captions and images for review, and schedules approved posts across compatible networks. TikTok and YouTube receive video posts only on Pro and Unlimited plans.

## Why This Matters

Most social media tools solve half the problem. Scheduling tools like Buffer and Later handle distribution but require you to create every post. Design tools like Canva help with visuals but don't write captions or decide what topics to cover. Boomp combines content strategy, copywriting, image generation, and scheduling into a single platform. The system uses your website, industry, and business profile to generate posts that match your specific services and voice, not generic templates. Each post is editable before it goes live, so you maintain full control without starting from a blank screen.

## Real-World Example

A two-location yoga studio connects its Instagram and Facebook accounts and shares its website. Boomp prepares 20 static posts covering class schedules, wellness tips, instructor spotlights, and seasonal promotions. The owner reviews and adjusts the batch, then approved posts are scheduled across both platforms for the month.

## What Most People Get Wrong

"Automated social media" usually triggers concerns about robotic, impersonal content. Boomp pulls context from your actual business (your services, location, industry language) rather than generating from generic prompts. The result sounds like a knowledgeable assistant who studied your website, not a chatbot recycling marketing templates.
