For every entrepreneur, there can almost be a very singular focus at play: working on your business and nothing else. Yes, you may be
working on protecting your assets
at the same time, but we have to remember that a full seven-day week is 168 hours, and therefore, we must think about everything that encompasses. We shouldn’t just focus on business skills or professional acumen, but recognize that life outside of your business and professional life is not just going to benefit you more, but can also aid your business practices, but how does the external environment affect this?

## The Rest and Recharge Components

If you are running a business from home, you need to make sure that there is a distinction between your work life and your home life, which can be tough. This is why learning how you can rest and recharge to serve you can make a big difference in your life and therefore your business.

The quality of your home is important. Lots of people invariably spend more time than they should when running their business, which is why the quality of your downtime is critical. Lots of people now opt for newly built homes rather than old fixer-uppers because, quite frankly, we don’t have the time to be dealing with putting up new wallpaper and those things that we all had time for 30 years ago. New-build properties are, of course, more energy-efficient, and as new homes require less maintenance, this means as an entrepreneur you can spend less time and money reducing your stress and aiding your growth mindset.

## Enhanced Innovation

Many people think that to run a business effectively, you’ve got to be mentally invested all of the time. We have to use this 168-hour model to our advantage. Look at just
how effective you truly are when you are burnt out!

Stepping away from your business routines can spark innovation and creativity because you are experiencing different environments, which can inspire new solutions and ideas. The more people recognize that getting away from business practices can actually aid their development, the better it can be in the long run.

## Stronger Relationships

We have to also consider that while spending time away from your business will actually increase your productivity when you return to work, it also gives you the opportunity to maintain a life outside of your business.

People become all consumed by their passions, for better or worse. We don’t have to talk about the clichés of business people who don’t spend enough time with their kids! Ultimately, strong support networks outside can benefit you then and serve as a foundation for your professional success.

By nurturing a life outside of your business, you’ll create a cycle that benefits your well-being
and
your entrepreneurial success.
