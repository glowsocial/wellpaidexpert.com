# Practical Ways To Beat The Odds As A Novice Entrepreneur

It’s well-documented that a high percentage of new businesses fail. Depending on which articles and reports you read, up to 90% of startups fold within the first five years of trading. These statistics may evoke anxiety if you’re an aspiring entrepreneur, but there are ways to beat the odds. In this informative guide, we’ll outline some practical ways to improve your chances of success as a new business owner.

Research the market

The first thing to do when you have an ingenious business idea or a desire to
launch your own company
is to research the market. Learn about competitors, define your target audience and get to know your ideal customer as well as possible. See what other products and services are available within the same categories and gauge the level of demand. If other businesses within the same industry are struggling and demand is falling, this is a warning sign. Try to identify opportunities within growing markets and tap into emerging trends. Use your research to develop ideas and make decisions that will help you attract customers, market your products effectively and build relationships with prospective customers. Everything about your brand should connect and resonate with your ideal client or buyer.

Take advantage of targeted tools and technology

Technology is advancing at pace, which is brilliant news for entrepreneurs. There are all kinds of tools, platforms, devices and software you can use to help you launch and run a successful business. From automated sales software and tools that enable seamless communications between business teams or solopreneurs and their clients, there are tools for every company. Tailor your investments to suit the type of business, your target market and your objectives. Look for targeted solutions within your industry that optimize efficiency and enhance customer experience. Online ordering is a brilliant example if you’re setting up a business like an online pharmacy. There are several benefits to recurring payments
for online pharmacies
including a stable, regular income for business owners, added convenience for customers and potential cost savings for people who need repeat prescriptions. If you’re not up to speed with the latest tech developments, research online and seek expert advice.

Prioritize money management

Cash flow problems are among the most common causes of
new business failure. The cost of launching a startup varies hugely, but prioritizing money management is beneficial for every entrepreneur. Whether you’ve invested capital or borrowed money, it’s critical to focus on your finances and analyze figures carefully. Take care when borrowing money and avoid trying to grow your business too quickly. Even if your products are flying off the shelves or your restaurant is fully booked most days, it’s wise to wait until you’re turning over a profit before expanding. Going too far too fast is risky. It’s also important to make sure the demand is there before you grow. If the demand for your products or services has already peaked, you may find that putting more money into that specific product or service results in losses. Try to be agile, encourage evolution and innovation and monitor emerging trends.
