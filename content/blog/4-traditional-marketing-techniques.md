While digital marketing has long been popular, it doesn’t mean that traditional marketing techniques don’t have their time and place. They could offer more results for companies in specific industries, especially when they’re looking for practical local marketing strategies.

And, these can often be some of the more cost-effective options to go for. Between their affordability and the return on investment they help generate, there’s no reason why you shouldn’t see better and better results as time goes on.

Four of these could be more than worth the investment.

## 1. Direct Mail

[Direct mail](https://www.modmail.com/blog/why-direct-mail-still-matters-in-a-digital-first-era) refers to mailing printed marketing materials directly to potential customers’ homes. This used to be a lot more popular, but it can still be an effective path to go down. It drives visibility a lot more than many alternatives, and this is especially true when you’re trying to drive local sales. 

You can take either a targeted or saturation approach for this. Targeted direct mail involves sending your marketing materials to a specific demographic, while saturated means sending them to every home in a local area. Both offer their pros and cons, depending on your goals.

## 2. Print Advertisements

While print publications aren’t as popular as they used to be, they’re still well-read around the world. Millions of people still trust these publications, especially when it comes to local newspapers and advertisements. That’s why [print advertisements](https://www.indeed.com/career-advice/career-development/print-advertising) can still be one of the more effective offline marketing strategies.

These are great for local reputation building and generating more brand awareness in a specific area. And, since these publications are more trusted, you’re more likely to generate trust from potential customers relatively quickly.

## 3. Referrals

Referrals involve getting your current or past customers to recommend your business to their friends and family, helping lead to more sales. This doesn’t happen by accident. It’s an area you’ll need to put time and effort into from the start. [Creating a referral program](https://www.salesforce.com/blog/referral-program-ideas-to-grow-your-business/) that actively encourages customers to recommend you is essential.

These referral programs don’t even have to cost as much as you’d think. It could simply involve offering a small discount on a new customer’s first purchase when they’ve been referred by an existing customer. Then, you can generate repeat business in time.

## 4. Event-Specific Marketing

Event-specific marketing focuses on interacting with potential clients and customers at various events, with this including trade shows, seminars, and conferences. But, you could also target more customer-focused or local events, like sports events, depending on what your business offers.

This has a direct impact on your sales and visibility, letting you directly interact with potential leads. Even getting feedback about your products or services during these events could help lead to more leads and sales as time goes on, making it more than worth it.

## Conclusion

Traditional marketing techniques could offer more results than many entrepreneurs realize, especially when it comes to local marketing. When combined with the right [digital marketing strategies](https://thewellpaidexpert.com/3-pillars-of-digital-marketing-success/), you should see more and more results, and income, as time goes on. They’re well worth it.
