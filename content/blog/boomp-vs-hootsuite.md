## Direct Answer

Hootsuite is a scheduling and analytics platform. Boomp is a content creation and scheduling platform. Hootsuite requires you to write every post. Boomp prepares posts from your business details for review, then schedules the posts you approve.

## Why This Matters

Hootsuite excels at team collaboration, approval workflows, and cross-platform analytics for marketing departments with 5-10 people. Its core assumption is that someone on your team is already creating the content. For a small business owner with no marketing team, this assumption breaks immediately. You sign up, connect your accounts, and face the same blank calendar you had before. Boomp starts from the opposite assumption: you have no time or desire to create content yourself. The platform prepares posts from your business context, lets you review them, and schedules the posts you approve. The trade-off is clear: Hootsuite gives a content team more distribution controls. Boomp gives a small business prepared content with less effort.

## Real-World Example

A four-person dental practice signed up for Hootsuite's $99 per month plan. After six weeks, they'd published three posts because the office manager (their designated "social media person") couldn't find time between patient scheduling and insurance calls. They switched to Boomp Core and received 20 dental-specific static posts covering oral hygiene tips, seasonal promotions, and new patient welcomes, ready to review and schedule.

## What Most People Get Wrong

Hootsuite isn't overpriced for what it does. It's the wrong tool for the wrong job. If you have a content team that produces daily posts and needs a distribution hub, Hootsuite is excellent. If you're a small business owner who needs posts created from nothing, you need a creation tool, not a distribution tool.
