Every business owner appreciates the importance of an efficient workforce. From recruitment drives to the right equipment, there are many contributing factors to consider. However, team building away days are often overlooked. Now is the time to give them the attention they deserve through the use of tactical activities.

From paintball to army simulations, there are plenty of experiences that fall into this category. Here’s why they are the ideal choice for your team.

## They emphasize safety

Tactical away days may sound counterproductive against health and safety. In reality, they highlight its importance while also making workers take it seriously. Whether it’s wearing a [tactical chest rig](https://www.bifrostgear.com/collections/chest-rigs-1) or surveying the area to look for hazards doesn’t matter. Individuals are continually paying attention to their safety. And how they interact with their surroundings.

This is a valuable skill that can subsequently support them in warehouses, offices, and shop floors alike. Outside of the occasional bruise or graze, injuries are very unlikely too.

## They encourage teamwork

Businesses often support employees on an individual basis. But [collective success](https://www.brighthr.com/articles/culture-and-performance/teamwork/the-importance-of-teamwork-in-the-workplace/) is the true hallmark of a great company. Virtually all tactical-based away day activities put workers into teams, forcing them to work as a team to achieve their goal. Yet, each member still has a defined role and must think about how their efforts integrate with team outputs.

The similarities with a thriving workplace are clear. Better still, employees will build connections with colleagues from other teams. This can support future communication and collabs.

## They support personal growth

Assembling the right team and adding talented candidates over time is a step in the right direction. However, the business will suffer if it [stops developing its own people](https://thewellpaidexpert.com/what-happens-when-a-business-stops-developing-its-own-people). Encouraging personal growth is the only way to keep your team ahead of the competition. Tactical away days challenge a person’s perceptions of themselves while also developing new skills.

Even when the newly acquired knowledge doesn’t directly relate to their role, it builds a sense of momentum. Follow this up with staff training endeavors linked to their jobs to see great results.

## They are a break from the norm

Some away days and team building exercises fall flat because they feel like another day at work. [Tactical adventures](https://campaign-paintball.com/briefing-room/corporate-away-day-ideas/) take teams out of their normal surroundings, creating a clear shift in mindsets. Crucially, teams become more engaged with the activities, thus gaining more from them. Of course, it will become a memorable event and an ongoing talking point for teams.

A break from the office or standard workplace also helps teams return with fresh energy. Many companies find that employees quickly find solutions to previous problems upon their return.

## They focus on taking action

Finally, this type of away day forces every member of the team to play an active role. There is no room for bystanders, spectators, or passive team members on the tactical battlefield. Employees have to take the initiative, make [quick decisions](https://www.iod.com/resources/business-advice/strategic-decision-making/), and take action. It is a high-pressured environment, albeit a fun one, that yields a big response.

Compared to alternative away days, this is easily one of the most beneficial. For small teams and large organizations alike, adding it to your itinerary is highly advised.
