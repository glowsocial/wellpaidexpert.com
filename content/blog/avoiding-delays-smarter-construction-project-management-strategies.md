Ask any company, regardless of the sector, and they will tell you delays are one of the costliest issues and challenges they face. For construction companies, delays can increase the cost of a project and
ruin your reputation
in seconds. Every setback will cost you time and money; whether it’s caused by supply chain issues, poor communication, or even issued permits, a setback is frustrating.

This is where effective project management comes into play. The better your project management and strategies for staying on time are, the smoother the process will be. And this post is going to dive into different ways you can keep things running on time and reduce delays and rising costs.

## Set Realistic Timelines

Overly optimistic timelines are going to be your biggest enemy here. Instead of trying to rush things, make it more realistic about how long each aspect of the project should take, and give more accurate timelines that have a degree of flexibility in them for unforeseen circumstances. When you underestimate timelines, you can create a domino effect whereby subcontractors are left hanging around or turning up on the wrong days, anticipating their part but not being able to move forward. A little buffer time means you avoid those issues and reduce problems from supply chain inspections and potential weather delays that can impede the project.

## Improve Communication

Poor communication can turn minor mishaps into huge delays, and when you’re working with multiple vendors, inspectors, and subcontractors, impeccable communication is essential to ensure everything runs smoothly and according to schedule.

A
centralised project management software tool
can increase effective communication between all parties and help you stay on track while delivering the right messaging to people when they need it. It allows you to keep everyone in the loop, conduct regular check-ins, enable document signing, and share progress updates and alerts for changes in plans, and has a single point of contact, making life easier for everyone.

## Secure Correct Permits

Permitting is a major headache for any company, and understanding what permits you need and obtaining them quickly is a challenge in and of itself. Local approval processes can be slow and cumbersome, and vary widely between districts. Missing even one small detail or document can seriously impact your entire project, leading to delays and costly changes.

## Plan for Risks

While you do need to hit your goals, which should be the main focus, a
good project manager will also plan for risks. Because if you know what needs to happen should plans go awry, then you can get back on track faster with improved plans and limited downtime while you reconfigure what you’re doing. Risks can include labor and supply shortages, increased costs, weather disruption, or unforeseen issues that will only become apparent once work starts. By having contingency plans in place, you can fall back on them when needed to stay on track, not grind to a halt.
