Growth is usually what every business is aiming for, but when it actually starts happening, it doesn't always feel as easy as you might have hoped - you'll end up with more inquiries, and that means more work, and that means you've got to do more in the same amount of time… And yes, at first that might feel quite positive, but then things get harder to manage and it could be that you start to make mistakes or miss things that need to be done. With that in mind, here are some tips on how to scale your business without overlooking the basics.

![Professionals collaborating around a conference table with scaling strategy on the screen](/scaling-business-without-overlooking-basics.png)

## Keep Your Processes Clear

As a business expands, it's easy for processes to get more complicated - you'll add extra steps, you'll start to use new tools, and before long, simple tasks start taking longer than they should, which can be a massive problem.

However, if you take a little time to review how things are done that can make a real difference, and a lot of the time, simplifying rather than adding is the best option, and clear, easy-to-follow processes also make it easier for others to step in and help when you need them too.

## Get Communication Right

Communication tends to happen fairly naturally in smaller teams, but growth can change that, and it might be that as you get bigger, it's harder for information to flow as easily, and it becomes easier for messages to get missed.

If you're able to [keep communication as straightforward as possible](https://www.helpguide.org/relationships/communication/effective-communication), that's going to help avoid confusion. A few ideas include regular updates, shared notes, or maybe just making sure everyone knows where key information is stored. It doesn't really matter how it's done, just that it is done and everyone knows where to find it.

## Stay On Top Of Compliance

When things get busy, compliance tasks tend to move down the priority list, but as your business grows, compliance becomes even more important, so you can't keep putting it off or hoping that it will just sort itself out.

And of course, not everyone is an expert in compliance and you might not have the time to keep up with all the changes, which is why this is one area where outsourcing makes a lot of sense. For example, you could find [health and safety services](https://www.opus-safety.co.uk/health-and-safety) to ensure you're keeping your staff, visitors, and customers safe, and that would go a long way to ticking the right boxes.

## Delegate Gradually

Scaling can mean [letting go of certain tasks](https://thewellpaidexpert.com/feeling-like-a-control-freak-business-owner-heres-how-to-let-go-without-costing-yourself-anything/) because you really don't have time for them anymore, or perhaps they've become more complicated and you don't have the knowledge needed to do them well - and there's no shame in that; no one can know how to do everything.

Trying to hold onto everything just means you're getting more stressed and more busy, so it's a good idea to delegate when it makes sense to. The best part is, when you delegate work, you're still in control, and you'll still have the final say, but you won't actually have to do the work anymore, so it's the best of both worlds.
