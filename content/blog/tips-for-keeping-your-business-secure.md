When you have a business, it’s vital you do what you can to keep it secure. Not doing so can mean that you put your revenue and company, let alone those that work for you at risk. One of the most important things when it comes to keeping your business secure is prioritizing your physical business
premises. You need to ensure it’s safe from things such as flooding and fires and there are many steps you can take to do this including removing any unsafe cladding, adding adequate draining and getting fire doors. You could also look into super safe external doors such as those sold on

## Offer regular training

Training
is imperative for keeping everyone in your business in the know. You want your employees to feel safe and know what to do in certain scenarios. Offer training in everything from fire safety, to what to do in the event of an emergency. They should also be regularly briefed on digital safety too, so as to recognize things like hacking attempts to their emails or dodgy websites which would harm your business. By offering regular refresher courses and handbooks that they can refer to whenever they might be wondering about something, it can keep your whole company safe and knowledgeable about what to do when.

## Conduct regular health and safety checks

As an employer, it’s your duty to keep your building safe from harm. To do this efficiently, ensure you conduct regular health and safety checks that can find any potential harm before it happens. These checks are designed to minimize any risks, identify where problems could arise in the future and to put plans in place should anything happen. These should be done at least annually, but more frequently if possible. This is particularly true if you have recently changed your equipment, moved your office around or have had an influx of new employees start with you.
