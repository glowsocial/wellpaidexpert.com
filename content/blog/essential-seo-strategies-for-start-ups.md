# Essential SEO Strategies for Start-ups

If you want to make sure that your
business
is getting off to a good start then it is so important that you use SEO to your advantage. If you are not using SEO then you may end up falling way behind your competition and this is the last thing you need. If you want to find out more then take a look below.

## SEO isn’t just for Google

One thing you need to take note of is that SEO isn’t just for Google. It’s so important that you look at the bigger picture. You also need to look at how long-term you would like your strategy to be. If you are in the early days of starting SEO then you can expect to wait six months or more before seeing results. A lot of small businesses make the mistake of thinking that they are going to see results overnight, but this is rarely the case. You need to try and lay the foundation first, and when you do, you can build on that for years to come. You should also be trying to target keywords that your customers are going to be searching for. Try and make sure that you understand their intent as well, as this will have a huge part to play when it comes to providing them with the best experience possible.

## Target the Right Keywords

It can be tempting for you to try and rank for single-term keywords but if you do then you may end up
burning yourself out
before you have even had a chance to begin. If you want to do something about this then you need to make sure that you are targeting the right keywords and with the right intent. If you can do this then you will soon find that it is easier than ever for you to get the result you need. You may also find that it is easier for you to stand out in the market as you can rank for longer-tail keywords, and then direct people to the exact thing that they are looking for. Although you will get fewer searches, your conversion rate will be very high, so be sure to keep this in mind as it will make a major difference to your strategy and your overall ROI.

## Focus on Website Design

It’s so important that you focus on website design as well. If your website design isn’t good then no amount of traffic is going to fix that. You need to have a solid foundation, and you also need to make sure that you are standing out from the crowd. If you need some help with your website design then there are a lot of services out there.
Adjust Web Design
is just one of them, and they are a great choice if you work within the healthcare sector. When you have perfected your website design, you can then focus on building keywords around your category pages, which will give you even more opportunities to rank.
