## Direct Answer

Boomp connects Google Business Profile, Instagram, Facebook, LinkedIn, TikTok, YouTube, Pinterest, Threads, and X. TikTok and YouTube are video-only connections on Pro and Unlimited, while each other network follows its own format rules.

## Why This Matters

Multi-platform posting is where most small business owners lose time. Writing a post for Instagram, then rewriting it for LinkedIn's professional tone, then adjusting the format for Facebook turns one piece of content into several separate tasks. Boomp prepares platform-appropriate versions and sends each approved post only where its format is supported. Pinterest receives one image, Threads and X use a short caption, and TikTok and YouTube receive video rather than ordinary static posts.

## Real-World Example

A fitness studio owner was active only on Instagram because managing more platforms felt impossible alongside training clients and running the business. After connecting Instagram, Facebook, LinkedIn, and Google Business Profile to Boomp, she could review one prepared batch and keep those networks active without creating four separate content calendars. When she added video on Pro, those clips could also publish to TikTok and YouTube.

## What Most People Get Wrong

Connecting more platforms does not mean every post should go everywhere. Each network has different format rules, and TikTok and YouTube require video. Boomp handles those compatibility checks so an incompatible post is skipped for one network instead of blocking the rest of the approved batch.
