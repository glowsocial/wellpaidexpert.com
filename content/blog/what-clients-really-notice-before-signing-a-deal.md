# What Clients Really Notice Before Signing a Deal

Clients really notice pretty much everything when you speak with them, invite them to the business or make changes to services. You only get one chance to make a great impression, so make it count. From empathy and understanding to valuable solutions, here are some ideas.

## The State of the Premises

Clients are always evaluating the partners they do business with. Through onsite visits to assess your suitability, clients will size up everything! Many enjoy getting to know partners and often request demonstrations and walk-throughs. This includes the interior and exterior of the premises, so you need to bring your A-game. Even before they walk inside, you must make an impression with something like
parking lot paving, clean courtyards, and even graffiti removal.

## An Empathetic Approach

Most clients will have specific needs, and they will come to you for a solution, but providing a solution is only part of the partnership. A valuable client usually also wants a partner who understands their business needs and ways in which to accommodate their unique position. With a complete understanding of a client’s issues, you can provide a solution that takes into account the pain points of their challenges and builds trust and loyalty from the beginning.

## Clients Really Notice Attention to Detail

It’s often the small things that make the biggest difference. As a B2B supplier, there are probably hundreds of others a client can turn to. But what makes them stay with your business is attention to detail. Around 60% of small businesses cite attracting new clients as the biggest marketing challenge, and there are
reasons you are struggling to attract clients. However, following through, tailored services, and clear communication make you the better choice.

## Solutions and Value of a Deal

Clients love partners who deliver results, and that’s pretty much all they want at the end of the day. It’s what they are paying for and what they expect. Value for money is factored into every decision a business makes, yours and theirs. B2B partners especially will always chase a good return on investment and a proactive approach to solving their problems. However, they also want a provider that offers exceptional service so they can build long-lasting relationships.

## A Long-Term and Flexible Commitment

Further to long-lasting relationships, clients are invested emotionally as well as financially and usually look for a partner who can commit to meeting their needs for the foreseeable future. When you
find a client that is a good fit, there is a good chance they will become partners for the lifetime of your business. To establish and build the trust necessary for this, it helps to be adaptable to their needs, deliver efficient services all the time, and collaborate when needed.

## Summary

How a business premises is presented is something clients really notice and make decisions on. Attention to detail in business is also something a client will look for and appreciate. However, they will also be willing to engage when you can commit to a long-lasting business partnership.
