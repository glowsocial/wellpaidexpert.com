If your ad campaigns don’t work the way you want them to, you can lose a lot of money very quickly. Most online ads are now expensive, and that means that you need a high return to break into this market. You can’t just wing it.

That’s where this post comes in handy. It explores how to fix your ads and ensure that you don’t wind up making costly mistakes. Here’s everything you need to know.

## Audit Your Goals

You’ll want to start by auditing your goals. What are you actually trying to achieve with your campaigns? A surprisingly high number of companies actually get into misalignment here, building ads that don’t reflect what it is that they ultimately want to do.

Therefore, make sure you’re clear on the outcome you’re looking for. Whenever you run an ad online, it should have some direct or indirect effect on your bottom line, enabling you to make a reasonable return.

## Pinpoint The Leakage In Your Funnel

It’s also a good idea to pinpoint the
leakage in your funnel. As prospects move from one step to the next on the road to conversion, it can be challenging to hold them so that they make it all the way through. That’s why it is critical to see how they move from one stage to the next.

Remember, even if your ad is highly effective at getting clicks, that doesn’t mean that you’ve solved all the problems. Some ads can be great, but if your backend isn’t set up for conversion, then the ROI will necessarily be low and you’ll wind up wasting a lot of money.

## Improve Audience Targeting

You also want to improve your audience targeting. If you can sharpen this up and make it work for you, then it can mean you get more dollars back for every one you spend.

How you improve audience targeting is very much up to you. The best way is to use a platform like
Scalable
to collect the data you need to make better decisions. Having AI on your side can help you determine where you need to deploy your resources, instead of having to rely on instinct and experience (which you may not have).

## Revamp Bad Ads

Of course, the solution may just simply be to revamp your bad ads. Sometimes, your marketing just sucks and your audience knows it. AI-generated ads with annoying animations and text are generally
unconvincing for the vast majority of individuals. They want something that they feel they can touch and believe in.

## Fix Technical Glitches

Finally, you’ll want to focus on fixing all the technical glitches that can come with ad positioning. If you have pixels that aren’t loading properly or trouble with images, it can lower your uptake and prevent your ads from being as compelling as you’d like. Therefore, check things like the UTM parameters and
implement a conversion API. These assist with compliances and also enable you to appeal rejections when they inevitably occur.
