# Your best bet is a
# scored quiz

Based on your answers, you should build a
scored quiz.
Think:

What is the environmental impact of your daily actions?

How well-traveled are you?

What's your sleep IQ?

I'd say that this is, by far, the most popular quiz to create. It's also the most similar to quizzes you'd take in SEVENTEEN magazine (not that I'd know anything about that!).

You set it up with a set number of end points, and then have options that correspond with each end point.

Then you score them, and voila! You're set.
