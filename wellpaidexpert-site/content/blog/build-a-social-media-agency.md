## Step 1: Choose Your Niche

The single biggest mistake new agency owners make is trying to serve everyone.

The best niches for social media management in 2026:

- **Local service businesses** (dentists, chiropractors, HVAC, plumbers, law firms)
- **Real estate agents and brokerages**
- **Restaurants and hospitality**
- **Health and wellness practitioners** (coaches, therapists, nutritionists)
- **E-commerce brands**

Pick one. Specialize. The agencies that scale fastest can say: "We specifically work with [niche] businesses."

---

## Step 2: Build Your Service Package

Keep your first offer simple:

- 15 to 20 posts per month
- Coverage across 2 to 3 platforms
- AI-generated captions, reviewed and approved before posting
- Monthly analytics summary

Price it between $750 and $1,200 per month. Do not custom-scope every engagement. Standardize the deliverables. Sell the same package until you have five clients, then add tiers.

## Step 3: Build Your AI Fulfillment System

With the right tools, you can generate a full month of content for each client in minutes.

The core stack:

- **AI writing tool** (ChatGPT or Claude) for caption generation
- **[Glow Social](https://glowsocial.com)** for multi-platform scheduling and analytics — connects to 12 platforms and automates posting entirely
- **Canva** for branded graphics

With this stack, serving one client takes 10 to 15 minutes per week of active work.

## Step 4: Get Your First Client

The most effective prospecting method for a new agency is the preview approach:

1. Choose 20 businesses in your niche that are posting inconsistently
2. Generate a sample social media feed for one of them using AI — 3 to 5 posts
3. Send a short LinkedIn message: "I made something for you — want to see it?"
4. Show the preview on a brief call
5. Close on the spot

## Step 5: Retain Clients and Grow

- **Consistent delivery** — posts go out on schedule, every week
- **Monthly reporting** — clients who see their progress churn at half the rate
- **Proactive communication** — reach out before they reach out
