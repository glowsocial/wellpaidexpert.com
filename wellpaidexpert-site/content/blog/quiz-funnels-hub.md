Quiz funnels are the highest-converting lead generation tool most experts are not using.

A standard lead magnet converts at 10 to 20 percent. A quiz funnel converts at 30 to 50 percent. The difference is personalization — people want their own results, not a generic PDF.

---

## Why quiz funnels outperform every other lead magnet

1. **Curiosity drives completion.** Quiz completion rates are 70 to 85 percent.
2. **Personalization increases trust.** A result that reflects your specific situation is more credible than broad advice.
3. **Segmentation improves follow-up.** Quiz answers reveal what problem each subscriber has, so your email sequence can speak directly to it.

---

## The four types of quiz funnels

**Personality quiz** — "What type of social media marketer are you?" Works for top-of-funnel awareness and social sharing.

**Scored assessment** — "How ready is your business for social media outsourcing?" Works for B2B, consultants, and anyone selling to buyers who are close to a decision.

**Knowledge trivia** — "How much do you know about email marketing?" Works for education businesses and authority building.

**Product recommendation** — "Which social media tool is right for your business?" Works for product-focused businesses and affiliates.

---

## How to build a quiz funnel in 5 steps

1. **Choose your type and topic** — match format to your goal; pick a topic that addresses the core problem your buyers face before finding you
2. **Write 5 to 10 questions** — conversational, easy to answer without research, designed to segment respondents
3. **Define 3 to 5 result segments** — each result should feel specific, accurate, and include a next step toward your offer
4. **Set up the email capture** — place it between the last question and the results page, framed as "Enter your email to see your results"
5. **Build the follow-up sequence** — 3 to 5 emails per result segment, delivering advice and introducing your paid offer
